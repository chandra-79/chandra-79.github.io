About Ziggle and the blog

Small daily puzzles, and honest writing about why practice works. Here is who is behind it and how we work.

Updated 23 November 2026

Who makes Ziggle

Ziggle is made and operated by Ajna CS, Singapore (ajnacs.com). The blog is written by the Ziggle team. You can reach us at info@ajnacs.com.

How we write the blog

  • Every number links to where it came from. Each post ends with its sources. Where a claim comes from a single study, a company or a Wikipedia summary, we say so.
  • No miracle claims. We never say a game will raise your IQ, rewire your brain, prevent memory decline or improve your health. Research on “brain training” is mixed, and we describe it that way.
  • Plain about our own games. When a post talks about a Ziggle game, it says what the game does and does not claim.
  • Pictures are drawn for Ziggle. The illustrations on posts are our own and are made from code; nothing is taken from other sites.

Corrections

If you find a mistake or a broken source, email info@ajnacs.com with the post’s address. We fix errors in the post and update its text; we do not quietly change what a post claims.
