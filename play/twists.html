    ARROW TWISTS

    Same arrows, new rules.

    • Tap an arrow to send it out the way it points. A blocked move costs one of three hearts.
    • Two-headed arrows have a head at each end: tap near the end you want it to leave from.
    • One-way tiles let an arrow cross only in the direction of their chevrons.
    • Colour gates stay shut until every arrow of their colour has left the board.
    • Faint lanes show where each end would go. Hint lights up an arrow that can leave now.

    Fifteen boards: three short lessons, then twelve that grow from 6×6 to 10×10. The daily Arrow Escape never uses these rules.
