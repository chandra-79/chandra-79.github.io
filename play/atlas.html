      HOW IT WORKS

      A map of English, and a reason to come back

      The Atlas keeps every word you meet in Word Cloud, Word Search, Letter Lock and Word Builder. Each one lights up in its region; words you have not met yet wait as dim shapes.

      Tap a region to see its words, and tap a word to see what it means. Come back each day to light up today’s word. The goal is 5,000 words, with marks along the way.

      Nothing here counts toward your streak or leaderboards, and nothing leaves this browser. It is just for you.
