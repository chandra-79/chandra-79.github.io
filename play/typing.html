      ACCURACY FIRST

      How the trainer works

      • Lessons climb ten levels, from the home row to code. In the first three, a new key opens only when every key so far is quick and accurate.
      • The cursor waits for the right key. A wrong key is marked and counts against accuracy, so mistakes never pile up.
      • A level passes when one run meets its speed and accuracy goal. Runs under 95% accuracy never count toward speed goals.
      • WPM counts five characters as one word. Raw WPM includes wrong keys. Steadiness shows how even your pace was.
      • The keyboard shows how each key is going. Turn it off in Settings to practise without looking.
      • Word Rain: type falling words before they land. Daily phrase: one sentence for everyone, every day.

      Your progress stays in this browser and in your Ziggle backups.
