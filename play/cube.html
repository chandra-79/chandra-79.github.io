        How the cube trainer works

        • Turn the cube: drag a sticker the way you want it to go, press a face letter (U D L R F B; Shift for anticlockwise; M E S slices; X Y Z whole cube), or open Move buttons. Drag the background, or use the arrow keys, to look around.
        • Learn climbs ten levels: notation, a first solve, the 2×2, the four-look last layer, F2L, full PLL and OLL, lookahead, big cubes and your own goals. Every level is open.
        • Algorithms shows every case with a picture. Mark the ones you know and they come back for review, spaced out over days.
        • Timer works with a real cube: WCA-style scrambles, 15-second inspection, averages of 5 and 12, and a metronome.
        • Free play: any size, scramble, undo, or type an algorithm to watch it.
        • Sticker labels (W Y R O B G) and the flat view help if colours are hard to tell apart.
