SUM SPARK

Chain your way to the target.

  • Tap a tile to start. Then pick an operator and the next tile: the total updates after every step, worked left to right like a calculator.
  • Each tile can be used once. Totals stay whole numbers from 0 to 9,999, so a division has to come out even.
  • Hit the target exactly for 100 points, or lock in your closest total: within 2 scores 60, within 5 scores 40, within 10 scores 20. Each hint costs 15.
  • Three rounds a day: Add up (+), Add and take away (+ −), and All four (+ − × ÷). There is always an exact answer.
  • Keys: 1–6 pick a tile, + − × (or * or x) ÷ (or /) pick the operator, Backspace undoes, Escape clears, Enter locks it in.

Everyone gets the same three rounds each day. Practice gives you as many more as you like.
