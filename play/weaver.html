    READ THE CLOUDS

    Find the hidden word.

    • Type straight into the big cloud. Its letter spaces show the word’s length.
    • Two clue clouds start open. Tap any closed cloud to flip it: each one says what kind of clue is inside. Meaning tells you what the word means, Similar gives a near-synonym, Opposite its reverse.
    • A wrong guess drifts down below the sky and tells you how many of its letters are in the right place.
    • You begin with 100 sparks. Each extra cloud costs 15, each revealed letter 20, each wrong guess 5. A solve is always worth at least 10.
    • Keys: type anywhere to start, Enter guesses, Escape clears.

    Everyone gets the same Word Cloud each day. Practice lets you play as many more as you like.
