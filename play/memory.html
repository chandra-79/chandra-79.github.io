A LITTLE PLAY. A LOT OF SPARK.

Meet the mischief.

  1. Watch. Remember the friends that light up, in order. A friend can appear more than once.
  2. Shuffle! The friends change places. Remember their faces, not just their spots.
  3. Recall. Tap the same friends in the same order. A Rewind round asks for the last one first!

There’s no countdown when you answer. A wrong tap lets you try that step again. Replay the whole trail whenever you need it.

Each round starts at 100 sparks. Mistakes cost 9–20 sparks, depending on trail length; replays cost 20. Five rounds make an adventure. Scores measure this game, so compare your own best in the same mode.

Play your way: tap, click, use the visible 1–6 keys, or Tab and Enter. Cozy has slower flashes and no reverse rounds. Sound is optional. New game lets you change modes.
