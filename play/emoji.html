        How the learner works

        • Levels go from faces and hearts to symbols, history and the flag of every country. Any level is open. A round is ten questions; 8 right earns its star.
        • Answer by tapping, or press 1–4. Press Enter for the next question.
        • Review brings back what you missed, then waits longer each time you get it right: a day, three days, a week, three weeks, two months.
        • Daily 10 is the same ten questions for everyone today. It never touches your game streak.
        • Browse shows every emoji, symbol and flag with its official name and code.
        • Emoji look different on every device: Apple, Google, Microsoft and Samsung each draw their own. Flags here are pictures, not your device’s emoji, so they look the same everywhere.

        Your progress stays in this browser and in your Ziggle backups.
