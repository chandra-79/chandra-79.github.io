FIVE KINDS OF SUDOKU

Fill every square.

  • Classic: every row, every column and every 3×3 box has the numbers 1 to 9, once each.
  • Addoku: Classic rules, plus the numbers inside each dashed cage add up to the small number in its corner. A number never repeats inside a cage.
  • Diagonal: Classic rules, plus each of the two long diagonals has 1 to 9, once each.
  • Mini 6×6: every row, every column and every 2×3 box has the numbers 1 to 6, once each.
  • Even / Odd: Classic rules, plus a round marker takes an even number (2, 4, 6, 8) and a square marker takes an odd number (1, 3, 5, 7, 9).

Every puzzle has exactly one answer. The numbers that come with the puzzle cannot be changed.

A number that breaks a rule is marked, for example a second 5 in a row. A number that is not marked only fits so far: it can still turn out to be wrong.

Notes let you pencil small numbers into a square while you decide. A hint fills one square correctly; you get three per puzzle.
