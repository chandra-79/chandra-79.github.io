FIVE KINDS OF SUDOKU

Fill the grid.

  • Classic: every row, column and 3×3 box holds 1–9 once.
  • Addoku: also, the digits in each dashed cage add up to its small number, with no repeats.
  • Diagonal: also, both long diagonals hold 1–9 once.
  • Mini 6×6: rows, columns and 2×3 boxes hold 1–6.
  • Even / Odd: round markers take even digits, square markers odd.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution. Notes let you pencil in candidates. A hint fills one cell correctly; you have three per puzzle.
