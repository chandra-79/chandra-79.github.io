      THE METHOD OF LOCI

      How the trainer works

      • Levels follow the memory-sport path: a linking story, your first palace, stronger pictures, word lists, names and faces, the Major system for numbers, numbers, cards, dates and abstract images, then competition sizes. Every level is open.
      • Study as long as you like; your time is kept for your records. Then recall without looking.
      • Review brings back word lists after 1, 3, 7, 21 and 60 days, and Major pegs you miss. Recalling beats rereading.
      • Daily 10 is the same ten words for everyone today. It never touches your game streak.
      • My palaces: build routes through places you know. They stay in this browser and in your backups, and are never sent anywhere.
      • Ziggle shows your own scores and makes no promises about your memory. The technique is old; the practice is yours.
