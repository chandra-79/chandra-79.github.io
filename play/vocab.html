      HOW IT WORKS

      A word a day, and a reason to come back

      Each day has one new word with its meaning, an example and how it sounds. Add it to My words to keep it.

      Practice asks five short questions: what a word means, which word fits a meaning, or which word fills a gap. Words you get right come back after a day, three days, a week and longer; words you miss come back tomorrow. Asking yourself is what makes words stick, so there is no need to reread lists.

      Nothing here counts toward your streak or leaderboards. It is just for you.
