Lose a level and a life lets you keep going: one more heart in the arrow games, one more guess in Letter Lock, or another go at a Sum Spark round. You get 3 free lives a day. After that, 50 ✦ credits buy one. Earn credits by playing: 20 for each daily, 50 for a perfect day, and 5 for each practice level (up to 50 a day). Credits can’t be bought. A daily finished with a life still counts for your streak, but it stays off the leaderboards.
