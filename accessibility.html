Accessibility

Play your way.

Updated November 14, 2026

What we have checked

Ziggle aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We are not there everywhere yet, and this page says what has and has not been checked.

Automated checks cover keyboard input in game flows, focus returning to play after dialogs, control labels, colour contrast rules, text-size rules, reduced-motion settings and small-screen layouts. Browser checks cover Chrome, Firefox and WebKit at desktop and phone-sized widths. These checks help find problems; they do not establish complete accessibility conformance.

We have not yet completed a manual screen-reader audit with VoiceOver, TalkBack or NVDA, or physical-phone checks. Browser emulation does not replace those checks. This statement will be updated as they are completed.

Controls and preferences

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls and Enter or Space to activate buttons. Individual games describe their keyboard controls in the How to play menu. Some games also require interaction with a visual board; please tell us if that prevents you from playing.

Settings → Play includes Calmer motion and play preferences. Ziggle also respects your device’s reduced-motion preference. Settings provides light and dark appearances. You can enlarge pages using your browser’s zoom controls.

First-time how-to-play examples have self-paced captions, a text alternative, Skip and replay. Calmer motion shows still steps. Temporary notices leave keyboard navigation when they expire; a focused action stays available until you move away or dismiss it with Escape.

Report a barrier

Email info@ajnacs.com, or use Settings → More → Feedback. Tell us which page or game you used, what you tried, what happened, and your browser or assistive technology if you know it. Include the help or alternative you need. Do not send sign-in codes, sync codes or private account details.

Ajna CS, Singapore, operates Ziggle. We use reports to reproduce barriers and prioritise fixes. We do not promise a fixed response or repair time.
