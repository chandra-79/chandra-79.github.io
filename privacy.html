YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICES

Privacy policy

Play without an account. Choose what follows you to other devices.

Updated 9 November 2026 · Applies to Ziggle’s website and apps

Who operates Ziggle

Ziggle is operated by Ajna CS, Singapore. Our company website is ajnacs.com. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or concerns about your data, email info@ajnacs.com.

What stays on your device

Your games, streaks, settings, lives and credits save in this browser or app. A second local copy helps recover progress if one local store is cleared. Downloaded backup files stay wherever you save them. Clearing all Ziggle site data removes the local copies; keep a backup if you want to return later.

Trainer progress (including the Word Builder’s word list and review dates) and the weekly word (your guesses and solved weeks) and the Lexicon Atlas (ziggle-atlas-v1: the words you have met, the day you first met each and which game it came from, kept only in this browser) and the surprises you have found (ziggle-eggs-v1: each sticker and the day you found it) and memory-palace room and spot names are included if you choose to back up or sync your progress. Picture notes entered while studying a memory palace are not saved. Up to 20 recent page-error notes stay on your device to help troubleshoot; Ziggle does not automatically upload them.

Optional online features

On this website, online features are switched off for now. Leaderboards, leagues, accounts, sync codes, feedback forms and email reminders are not available here, so the parts of this page about them do not apply yet. Your games and settings stay in this browser.

  • Leaderboards: posting a daily result shares a random player ID, your chosen public name and the result needed to check your score. Other players can see your name and result, but not that ID. Scores are retained for 90 days.
  • Friend leagues: the server stores the league name, members’ player IDs and public names until the last member leaves. Anyone with the league code can view its standings. Avoid using your real name or private information in a public name.
  • Sync codes: the server stores a copy of your backup under a hash of the code, for 180 days after its last use. Anyone who has the code can access that copy. Treat it as a secret.
  • Moderation: to keep a reported name hidden, the server can retain a player ID or league code and the date of the decision until that decision is removed. This hides the public name without removing scores or league membership.
  • Feedback: messages sent through Settings include the topic and page, and are retained for 180 days. Ziggle does not attach your account, player ID or network address. Please leave out sensitive information.

Pending online requests may wait on your device after a connection or temporary server failure. They can include leaderboard results or an optional account backup and device token. The queue holds at most 20 requests for up to two days. Kids mode pauses retries; account retries merge current progress before sending.

Email accounts

An account is optional. If you sign in, Ziggle sends a six-digit code to your email address through Resend. Codes expire after ten minutes. The server uses a hash of your email address to find your account and keeps a masked address, your saved progress, and up to ten device names with sign-in and last-use times. Device sign-in tokens are stored as hashes on the server. Your signed-in device keeps your email address and its token locally.

Accounts are retained for 400 days after their last use, unless you delete yours sooner. Signing out removes that device’s access; it does not delete the account or progress on other devices.

The weekly email

The weekly note is off unless you switch it on in Settings → Saving. When enabled, Ziggle also stores your full email address and sends an email on Sundays with blog posts and, when available, your streak and leagues. It records the week sent so the same note is not sent repeatedly.

Switch it off in Settings → Saving or use the unsubscribe link in an email. Ziggle then removes the full email address from the active account record and stops weekly emails. You can still request a sign-in code.

Resend receives the recipient address and message content to deliver sign-in and weekly emails. Its handling and retention of delivery data are described in Resend’s privacy policy at resend.com/legal/privacy-policy. This page loads no content from Resend.

Email delivery data is processed by Resend in the United States. Service providers may process data outside Singapore or your country. We use providers to operate the features you choose, not to sell your information. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the service and its users.

Reminders

In a Ziggle app that supports device notifications, you can choose a daily reminder time and grant notification permission. The schedule stays on your device; no push service receives it. Turn it off in Ziggle’s settings or your device’s notification settings. Browser versions do not currently provide these local app reminders.

A separate daily email signup may be offered when a mailing provider is configured. It is optional and sends your address and consent to that provider, which manages the subscription and unsubscribe links. If the service is unavailable, no subscription is created. The weekly account email is a separate choice.

Counts, cookies and promotional cards

Ziggle pages use no cookies, third-party scripts, advertising trackers, outside fonts or outside content servers. Day-level visit counts are enabled by default; they record totals rather than a history tied to a person, such as how many visits there were today and how many came from people who first visited a week ago. These totals are retained for 400 days. Turn them off in Settings → Saving. They are also disabled with Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control or kids mode.

Promotional spaces show Ziggle’s own cards and, some days, a card marked “Ad” that links to another site we run. Cards are plain links: nothing is loaded from the other site until you tap, and the link carries only a campaign name, never an ID, name or anything about you. Cards are chosen by date, not by your activity. Kids mode hides them all.

Kids mode and bundled apps

Kids mode is paused for now: its switch is not shown in Settings and nothing in Ziggle is aimed at children. Existing saved progress remains on the device. An app built without a Ziggle server also hides online features and sends no data through them. Web play requires an internet connection to open pages and fresh puzzle content. Saved progress stays on this device. Bundled app builds retain their local assets; server-disabled builds send nothing through online features.

Storage and recovery copies

Ziggle uses network addresses temporarily in memory to limit abusive requests; the application does not store them on disk. Account and sync data are not published. Server recovery snapshots contain copies of the same records, with up to seven daily snapshots retained. Removed records may remain in those copies until the snapshots are replaced. Backups you download remain under your control.

Your choices and deleting data

Use Settings → Saving → Delete my account to remove your active server account and its saved progress. This also ends its weekly email and signs out its devices. Progress already saved on your devices remains, and separate sync copies, posted scores and league memberships are not removed by deleting an account.

You can leave leagues, stop using a sync code, turn off visit counts and reminders, and clear Ziggle’s site data on each device. Download a backup first if you want to keep your games. Public scores and anonymous totals follow the retention periods above.

Questions and changes

Contact info@ajnacs.com to ask about your data, request access or correction, withdraw consent for an optional feature, or raise a privacy concern. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify that a request concerns your data. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or sync codes. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide the feature that needs it.

For a general product problem, you can also open Settings → More → Feedback and choose “Something else.” Feedback is not an identity-verification channel; use the account controls above or the contact email for account requests.

Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date. See the Terms of Use for the rules of play.
